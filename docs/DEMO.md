# Demo

[Product walkthrough — 4:03](demo/walkthrough.mp4)

1. Start at Profile with no confirmed conversation details.
2. Discuss a fictional engineering background, career goals and working preferences.
3. Review the agent's proposed details and their supporting quotes.
4. Correct the target-role wording, confirm the selected details and save.
5. Return to Profile, then explore the fictional opportunity and application workflow.

The candidate's spoken input is scripted and synthesized with a stock voice. Agent responses and extraction are live. OpenScreen captures and exports the interface; the browser's actual call audio is synchronized separately. Optional captions are based on the session transcript.

The recorded call completed without interrupted spoken turns. Five confirmed facts persisted after reload. The opportunity workflow uses fixtures, not the newly confirmed profile. No employer submission occurs.

Screenshots and recording were reviewed for personal information. The video also received sampled OCR checks; these checks are not a guarantee against every possible disclosure. Small presentation improvements may appear in source after recording.
